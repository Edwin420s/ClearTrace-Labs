---

## Slide 2: 01 / THE PROBLEM
### Disagreement is expensive. Uncertainty is even worse.
Organizations often have the records—but not a trusted way to resolve what they mean.

* **💰 MONEY LEAKS:** Invoices, purchase orders, and delivery notes tell different stories.  
  *Example: KSh 5,000 potential variance on a single order.*
* **🤝 TRUST BREAKS:** Partners dispute ownership, terms, or what was actually agreed.  
  *Weeks of back-and-forth email arguments.*
* **⏳ DECISIONS STALL:** Teams chase emails and spreadsheets instead of finding the missing proof.  
  *Slow, hard-to-audit outcomes.*

> **The Gap:** A comparison can show a mismatch. It rarely tells people what evidence would settle it—or how to defend the final call.

---

## Slide 3: 02 / THE MOMENT
### A small mismatch can become a costly decision.
*Example: A procurement team is asked to pay an invoice that does not match the purchase order.*

| Purchase Order | Supplier Invoice | Potential Variance |
| :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **90 units** | **100 units** | **KSh 5,000** |
| Authorized order | Amount requested | 10 extra units × KSh 500 |

*But the mismatch alone does not prove fraud—or prove the invoice is wrong.*  
Was there an approved amendment? Were 100 units delivered? Which document governs payment?  
**The right next step is to identify the evidence needed, not guess.**

---

## Slide 4: 03 / THE SOLUTION
### ClearTrace turns conflicting records into a clear investigation.
One workspace to understand the disagreement, find what is missing, and record a defensible outcome.

1. **Bring the records together:** Upload relevant documents, claims, or partner statements.
2. **See what agrees—and what doesn’t:** Separate supported facts, contradictions, and unknowns.
3. **Find the next best evidence:** Get focused requests that can move the case forward.
4. **Decide with accountability:** A human reviews the evidence, records a reason, and exports the trail.

> **ClearTrace does not manufacture consensus. It makes uncertainty visible and resolution actionable.**

## Slide 7: 06 / BUSINESS MODEL
### Charge for reliable resolution—not another AI seat.
A SaaS model aligned with the cost of disputes and the value of a defensible decision.

* **Team Tier:** Monthly subscription for small teams testing a repeatable workflow. Limited cases and users, standard exports.
* **Business Tier:** Workspace subscription for operations teams handling recurring disputes. Higher case volume, audit history, review workflows.
* **Enterprise Tier:** Annual contract for regulated or multi-entity organizations. Role-based access, retention controls, ERP integrations.
* **Pay-Per-Reconciliation API:** Micro-billing ($0.05 per verification) for automated invoice and marketplace workflows.

## Slide 8: 07 / GO-TO-MARKET
### Win trust in one workflow, then expand.
1. **Design Partners:** Recruit 3–5 teams with frequent invoice, contract, or delivery disputes. Signal: users bring real cases and return.
2. **Paid Pilots:** Run a time-boxed pilot with a defined workflow and baseline. Measure resolution time, escalations, and evidence completeness.
3. **Repeatable Sales:** Package onboarding, security answers, and case templates. Convert successful pilots into annual contracts.
4. **Expand Accounts:** Add teams, workflows, and integrations after demonstrated value.

## Slide 9: 08 / TRUST & SAFEGUARDS
### Make the decision explainable—and keep humans accountable.
For high-stakes disputes, the right product is not an automatic verdict. It is a better decision process.

* **Evidence Before Conclusion:** Show what each source supports and where accounts conflict.
* **Uncertainty Stays Visible:** Label unresolved claims; never disguise missing evidence as certainty.
* **Human Review:** Authorized reviewers choose the outcome and record their rationale.
* **Traceable Record:** Preserve case history and provide cryptographic integrity checks for exported evidence.
